# Mortgage (crédito habitação) as a self-employed foreigner

> **Applies to:** a resident trabalhador independente (Categoria B), often with foreign clients, buying a home in Portugal with a Portuguese bank loan
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-02 — Banco de Portugal tightened the rules on 1 Aug 2026; the under-35 state guarantee is due to end on 31 Dec 2026

Portuguese banks lend to freelancers. They underwrite you on **declared** income — what's on your IRS notas de liquidação — not on invoices or bank inflows. They apply the Banco de Portugal's borrower limits on top of their own policy. The work is mostly paperwork you already produce: two years of IRS, clean AT and SS standing, a credit-register extract and a valid residence card. This file covers the limits, the under-35 guarantee and tax exemptions, what banks ask for, and the sequence to the escritura.

## Rules

### Banco de Portugal limits (Recomendação Macroprudencial n.º 1/2026, from 1 Aug 2026)

| Limit | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **LTV** — loan ÷ min(price, appraisal) | **≤ 90%** for habitação própria e permanente (HPP); **≤ 80%** for other purposes (second home, buy-to-let) | art. 5.º *(official source)* |
| **DSTI** — all monthly credit instalments ÷ net monthly income | **≤ 45%** (was 50% until 31 Jul 2026). Each bank may exceed it on **up to 10%** of the amount it lends per semester, with justification | art. 6.º *(official source)* |
| **Maturity** | **≤ 40 years** if the borrower is ≤ 35; **≤ 35 years** if over 35. With several borrowers, the **oldest** one's age counts | art. 7.º *(official source)* |
| **Interest-rate shock in DSTI** | for **variable or mixed-rate** loans, the new instalment is stressed by **+1,5 p.p.** if the term is over 10 years, **+1 p.p.** for 5–10 years, **+0,5 p.p.** up to 5 years | art. 4.º n.º 2, via BdP Instrução 23/2023 *(official source: BdP communiqué on the revised Instrução, Oct 2023)* |
| **Income after 70** | if the borrower is over 70 at the loan's end, income in the DSTI is cut by **≥ 20%, weighted by the share of the term past 70**. Not applied if already retired. With joint income, the older borrower's age counts | art. 4.º n.º 5 b) *(official source)* |
| **Bank-owned properties** | no longer eligible for 100% LTV; general 90/80% limits apply | preamble *(official source)* |

These are **recommendations** on a comply-or-explain basis: banks report every loan and must justify deviations, and BdP may take other measures if it rejects the justification *(official source: Recomendação, preamble and art. 10.º)*. That BdP may make them binding is *(unverified — ECO, Jul 2026)*. In practice they are the ceiling.

**What DSTI means for a freelancer:** BdP defines income as net annual income (after tax and SS) from the **last tax return** and/or the income of the **3 months before** the assessment *(official source: Recomendação art. 2.º i))*. Banks often average two years of liquidações, and a good current year that isn't on a nota yet usually doesn't count. *(unverified — common bank practice)*

### The under-35 state guarantee (100% financing) — DL 44/2024

- **What:** a personal state guarantee of **up to 15% of the transaction value**. It lets the bank lend above the 90% LTV, up to 100% of the price. *(official source: DL 44/2024 art. 2.º f)–g))*
- **Deadline:** contracts **signed by 31 Dec 2026**. No extension had been announced as of Sep 2026. The fund was topped up by €750 M in Apr 2026, but that added money, not time. *(official source: BdP Portal do Cliente Bancário for the date; top-up and no-extension: secondary, Sep 2026)*
- **Eligibility** *(official source: DL 44/2024 art. 2.º; portugal.gov.pt "Garantia Pública: o que é e como funciona" for the no-debt condition)*:
  - every borrower aged **18–35**, with **domicílio fiscal in Portugal**;
  - **first** HPP;
  - transaction value **≤ €450 000** (taken as the price, or the appraisal if lower — *secondary: BPI*);
  - income **within the 8.º escalão IRS** — rendimento coletável ≤ **€86 634 (2026)** *(official source: CIRS art. 68.º, Lei 73-A/2025)*;
  - no urban residential property owned;
  - guarantee not used before;
  - **no debts to AT or SS**.
- **Foreigners** qualify on the same terms. The test is fiscal domicile, not nationality. *(official source: DL 44/2024 art. 2.º a) sets no nationality condition)*
- Your bank must be a signatory: the State signed guarantee contracts with 19 credit institutions *(official source: Conta Geral do Estado 2025)*. Banks aren't obliged to lend even when you qualify, and your bank must still have allocation. *(unverified — BPI and other secondary sources)*

### IMT and Imposto do Selo exemption for young buyers — DL 48-A/2024

- **Who:** buyers **≤ 35** at the date of the purchase, not a dependent for IRS that year, buying their **first HPP**. Excluded if you own (or owned, in the previous **3 years**) any urban residential property or part of one. *(official source: CIMT art. 9.º n.ºs 2–3)*
- **2026 thresholds (Continente):**
  - **full exemption** from IMT and from the Imposto do Selo on the purchase (0,8%) up to **€330 539**;
  - partial exemption up to **€660 982**.

  *(official source: CIMT art. 17.º n.º 1 b), Lei 73-A/2025; IS verba 1.1 per AT "IMT Jovem" page. Açores/Madeira thresholds are higher — secondary)*
- **Not covered:** the Imposto do Selo on the **loan** (0,6% on terms ≥ 5 years, TGIS verba 17.1.3). The youth exemption covers only verba 1.1 (the purchase). *(official source: TGIS; AT "IMT Jovem" page)*
- No end date, unlike the guarantee. *(official source: CIMT art. 9.º has no sunset clause)*

### What banks and brokers ask a self-employed applicant for

*(unverified — typical bank and broker checklists; each bank differs)*

- **Identity and status:** passport, **residence card** (or CRUE for EU citizens) with enough validity left, NIF, NISS.
- **Income:** the **last 2 Modelo 3 declarations with their notas de liquidação** (`certidoes.md`), proof that your activity is open (início de atividade, Situação Fiscal Integrada), and this year's recibos verdes / SIRE list.
- **Standing:** AT não-dívida certidão and SS declaração de situação contributiva (both valid 4 months), and the **Mapa de Responsabilidades de Crédito** — see [`certidoes.md`](certidoes.md).
- **Banking:** **3–6 months of statements** of the account that receives your income, plus statements for existing loans.
- **Property** (later): CPCV, caderneta predial, certidão permanente do registo predial, licença de utilização or exemption, ficha técnica.

**How foreign-client income is viewed** *(unverified — broker practice)*:

- It counts if it's on your Portuguese IRS. Banks don't treat it differently because the client is abroad.
- They may ask for client contracts to judge stability.
- Income received in another currency, or into a foreign account, may be haircut or need extra proof.
- Concentration on one client is a risk flag.
- Under 2 years of activity is a common rejection reason.

### Credit intermediaries (brokers)

- Anyone who intermediates mortgage credit needs **Banco de Portugal authorisation and registration** (DL 81-C/2017). Check the broker's name in BdP's list of authorised credit intermediaries before signing anything. *(official source: BdP Portal do Cliente Bancário)*
- Ask in writing whether the broker is **tied (vinculado)** or **not tied (não vinculado)**. The law fixes who pays: a **tied** broker (or one acting a título acessório) is paid **only by the lenders** and may take nothing from you (art. 58.º). A **non-tied** broker is paid **only by you** and may take nothing from lenders (art. 61.º). Before starting, the broker must give you, on paper or another durable medium, the pre-contract information document (art. 54.º). A non-tied broker giving advice must state its fee, or how it's calculated, before advising. *(official source: DL 81-C/2017 as reproduced in BdP's "O regime dos intermediários de crédito" brochure, 2019 — check for later amendments)*

### Consumer protections in the loan process — DL 74-A/2017

- Before the contract, the bank gives you the **FINE** (Ficha de Informação Normalizada Europeia) and the minuta.
- The offer **binds the bank for at least 30 days**.
- You **cannot accept it during the first 7 days**: the period of reflection. A guarantor gets the same 7 days.

*(official source: DL 74-A/2017 art. 13.º n.ºs 3–6)*

## How to do it

```
1. Pre-check          2. Pre-approval        3. Property + CPCV       4. Appraisal          5. Approval → FINE        6. Escritura
   (you, 1–2 wks)        (bank/broker,           (sinal paid,             (bank-appointed,      (≥ 7-day reflection,       (deed + loan contract
                          ~1–2 wks)               deadline set)            fee paid by you)      ≥ 30-day binding offer)    signed same day)
```

1. **Pre-check your own file** — before talking to a bank:
   - last 2 IRS notas filed and paid;
   - no AT or SS debt;
   - Mapa CRC clean;
   - residence card valid well past the expected escritura date — see [`residency-aima.md`](residency-aima.md);
   - this year's declarações trimestrais and PPCs up to date.
2. **Pre-approval (aprovação de princípio / simulação):** compare at least two banks, or use one registered broker. Test DSTI at 45% including the rate shock.
3. **CPCV (promissory contract) and sinal (deposit):** make the CPCV conditional on loan approval, or keep the escritura deadline long enough (60–90 days) for the bank. *(unverified — practice)*
4. **Avaliação:** the bank's appraiser values the property. LTV is computed on the **lower** of price and appraisal.
5. **Final approval → FINE + minuta.** Read the FINE: TAEG, spread, fixed/mixed/variable, mandatory insurances (life + multirisk — you may buy them elsewhere), early-repayment fees. Wait out the 7 days.
6. **Escritura:** deed and loan contract signed together, at a notary or Casa Pronta. IMT and Imposto do Selo are paid beforehand unless exempt. Re-pull any certidão that has expired by then.

**Timeline:** about 6–12 weeks from pre-approval to escritura is commonly quoted. *(unverified)* Leave room for the guarantee's 31 Dec 2026 contract deadline.

## Gotchas

- **The bank reads your notas de liquidação, not your recibos.** If last year's IRS isn't filed and liquidated, that income barely exists for underwriting. File early in April when you plan to buy.
- **Low declared income cuts both ways.** Expenses that reduced your taxable income — or a very low rendimento coletável — also cut what you can borrow.
- **An unpaid SS month or PPC kills the garantia pública** (no-debt condition) and shows on the certidões. Clear everything before applying.
- **Certidões expire mid-process** (4 months). Re-pull them before the escritura.
- **Over-35 co-borrower = 35-year cap.** The oldest borrower sets the maximum maturity.
- **31 Dec 2026 is a signing deadline, not an application deadline.** Approvals in December can miss it.
- **Residence card renewal during the process:** some banks won't sign on an expired card plus renewal receipt. *(unverified)* Time the renewal.

## Typical document checklist for a self-employed applicant

What brokers and banks typically ask a recibos-verdes applicant for, with the self-employed substitute for each employee document. *(verified in practice)*

| # | Asked for | Self-employed version |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ID | Passport photo page + residence card front and back |
| 2 | Last 3 payslips | Screenshot of Portal → Faturas e Recibos Verdes list with **your name visible top-left**, covering ~12 months, plus the recibo PDFs |
| 3 | Proof of employment | Declaração de Início de Atividade (+ any Alteração), plus a client contract if you have one |
| 4 | Last rent receipt | Portal → Arrendamento e-receipt; if the landlord stopped issuing them, bank statements prove payment |
| 5 | IRS | Last two Modelo 3 declarations + notas de liquidação; add any payment-plan approval |
| 6 | Bank statements | Last 3 months — income credits and savings |
| 7 | Credit register | Banco de Portugal Mapa CRC (`../portals/other-sites.md`) |
| 8–10 | Clean standing | AT certidão de não dívida · SS declaração de situação contributiva · certidão de domicílio fiscal (`certidoes.md`) |
| 11 | No other property | Certidão predial negativa |
| 12 | Residency | Residence card (replaces the earlier AIMA approval declaration once issued) |
| 13 | Data consent | The broker's signed RGPD form |

- **Assemble it close to submission.** AT and SS certificates last about 4 months; bank statements and recibo screenshots go stale monthly.
- **Share the package to named people only.** A folder left on "anyone with the link" is a full identity kit.
