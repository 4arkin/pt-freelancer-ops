# Certidões and proof documents

> **Applies to:** anyone resident with a NIF and NISS — especially trabalhadores independentes facing a bank, AIMA, a landlord or a public client
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-02

Banks, AIMA, landlords and public bodies all ask for the same small set of proofs: no tax debt, no SS debt, your address, your income, your credit history and, sometimes, your criminal record. Almost all are free, online and instant. The traps are validity windows (a certidão that expires mid-process gets rejected) and asking for the wrong document.

## Rules

### Master table

| Document | Issuer | Click path | Cost | Validity | Typical requester |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Certidão de dívida e não dívida** (situação tributária regularizada) | AT | Portal → search "Certidão de dívida" → Pedir Certidão → **Dívida e Não Dívida** → CONFIRMAR → CERTIDÃO | free | **4 months** *(official source: AT folheto, Feb 2026)* | bank (mortgage), garantia pública jovem, public contracts, AIMA |
| **Declaração de situação contributiva** | Segurança Social | SSD → **Pagamentos e dívidas → Situação contributiva → Declaração da situação contributiva** | free online | **4 months** *(official source: ISS Guia Prático; gov.pt, Sep 2026)* | bank, AIMA, public clients |
| **Certidão de domicílio fiscal** (morada) | AT | Portal → search "Certidão de Domicílio Fiscal" → Pedir Certidão → **Domicílio Fiscal (Morada)** → CONFIRMAR → OBTER. Also in English | free | not stated by AT *(official source: AT folheto, Feb 2026, gives validity only for não-dívida and caderneta)*. Requesters usually want one ≤ 3 months old *(unverified — practice)* | bank, foreign clients, AIMA, utilities |
| **Certidão de residência fiscal** | AT | Pedir Certidão → **Residência Fiscal** (also in English; can be issued per income year) | free | not stated by AT *(official source: AT folheto, Feb 2026)*. It attests residence for the year it names, so pull one per income year *(unverified — practice)* | **foreign clients**, to apply a double-tax treaty and skip withholding |
| **Certidão de liquidação de IRS** | AT | Pedir Certidão → **Liquidação de IRS** → year → OBTER | free | — | bank, AIMA (means of subsistence), landlord |
| **Comprovativo da declaração Modelo 3** | AT | search "Comprovativo IRS" → **Obter Comprovativos** → year (last 5 years; only the operative declaration) | free | — | bank (2 last years), AIMA |
| **Proof of open activity** (início / atividade exercida) | AT | Situação Fiscal Integrada → Atividade Exercida, printed *(verified in practice for viewing; the print/PDF route is unverified)* | free | — | bank, AIMA (art. 89.º permits) |
| **Certidão predial negativa** (you own no property) | AT | Pedir Certidão → **Predial Negativa** (individuals only) | free | not stated by AT *(official source: AT folheto, Feb 2026)* | IMT Jovem / first-home claims, social support, some banks |
| **Certidão predial** (property you own) | AT | Pedir Certidão → **Predial** | free | — | bank, AIMA (address by ownership) |
| **Caderneta predial urbana** | AT | search "Caderneta predial urbana" → Consultar Património Predial → CADERNETA | free | **12 months** *(official source: AT folheto)* | bank (the property being bought or mortgaged) |
| **Certidão permanente do registo predial** | IRN (Conservatória) | predialonline.pt / Balcão Único | €15 online, €20 at a desk *(unverified — Santander and accountant guides; no IRN price page could be fetched)* | **6 months**, always up to date *(official source: gov.pt "Consultar uma certidão permanente do registo predial"; Portaria 1513/2008)* | bank and notary at escritura |
| **Mapa de Responsabilidades de Crédito** (CRC) | Banco de Portugal | bportugal.pt/area-cidadao/formulario/227 → accept conditions → **obter mapa** (optionally a past month) → log in | free | snapshot of the **previous month**; requesters usually want the latest | every bank and credit broker |
| **Certificado de registo criminal** (PT) | DGAJ / Justiça | Registo Criminal Online (via gov.pt "Pedir o Registo Criminal de pessoas"), with CMD | **€5** *(official source: DGAJ)* | **3 months** *(official source: gov.pt, Sep 2026)* | employers, some public clients. AIMA usually consults it itself, with the authorisation in your application |
| **Comprovativo de IBAN** | your bank | homebanking → account details → "comprovativo de IBAN" / "declaração de titularidade" | free in most banks *(unverified)* | — | AT/SS IBAN changes, clients, employers |
| **Atestado de residência** | Junta de Freguesia | in person or the Junta's site; usually two witnesses or a lease | set by each Junta *(unverified)* | usually 3–6 months *(unverified — no national rule found)* | AIMA (address proof — listed on its EU-citizen permanent-residence pages *(official source: AIMA)*; for third-country renewals AIMA asks for a sworn declaration plus landlord declaration or registo predial instead), CRUE at the Câmara |

All AT certidões carry a **validation code**. A third party can verify one at Portal → Cidadãos → Serviços → Certidões e Comprovativos → **Validar Documento** with your NIF and the code, so you can send the code instead of a scan. *(official source: AT folheto "Certidões e comprovativos", Feb 2026)*

### Alternatives to handing over a certidão

- **AT — Autorizações de Consulta de Dados:** Portal → Todos os Serviços → Autorizações de Consulta de Dados → Autorizar → Nova Autorização → the entity's NIF → Confirmar. The entity then checks your tax standing directly. *(unverified — Santander, Conselhos do Consultor)*
- **AIMA** checks the AT and SS databases itself for renewals. The CPLP renewal FAQ says no declaração negativa is needed. Applicants still often bring both to cover any database gap. *(official source: AIMA FAQ AR CPLP renovação; the "bring them anyway" advice is unverified practice)*

### What "regularizada" means

- **SS:** no debt, **or** a debt in a prestações plan being honoured, **or** a debt that is suspended or contested with a guarantee. *(official source: ISS Guia Prático "Declaração de Situação Contributiva")*
- **AT:** a payment plan being honoured doesn't block the não-dívida certidão. *(verified in practice)* Plan rules: [`at-communication.md`](at-communication.md#tax-debts-plano-prestacional).
- A coima or an unpaid PPC can make either certidão show debt. Clear them before a mortgage or renewal. *(unverified — general rule)*

## How to do it

1. **Bank or AIMA asks for "certidões"** → pull AT não-dívida + SS situação contributiva on the **same day**, close to submission. Both expire after 4 months.
2. **Save with the date in the filename**: `YYYY-MM-DD_certidao-at-nao-divida.pdf`, `YYYY-MM-DD_ss-situacao-contributiva.pdf` (see [`storage/filing-structure.md`](../storage/filing-structure.md)). Note the expiry date in the log.
3. **Mortgage pack:** add Mapa CRC, the last two Modelo 3 comprovativos with their notas de liquidação, and domicílio fiscal. The full list is in [`mortgage-self-employed.md`](mortgage-self-employed.md).
4. **Foreign client withholding tax** → send the certidão de residência fiscal (English version) before the first invoice of each year. US payers ask for their own form **W-8BEN** instead (the treaty-form route in `foreign-income-and-accounts.md`). *(unverified — standard US payer practice)*
5. **CRC login fails** → accept the cookie banner first. *(verified in practice — [`portals/other-sites.md`](../portals/other-sites.md#banco-de-portugal--mapa-de-responsabilidades-de-crédito-crc))*

## Gotchas

- **The AT não-dívida certidão lasts 4 months, not 3.** AT's Feb 2026 leaflet says 4. Older guides and some requesters still say 3 — if they insist on 3, pull a fresh one.
- **A long process outlives its certidões.** Mortgage approval to escritura can take months. Expect to re-pull AT, SS and the CRC before signing.
- **"Certidão predial negativa" means two different things.** AT's (no property in your name, free) is not the IRN registo predial document for a specific property. Ask which one.
- **The CRC covers the previous month.** A loan you just signed won't show yet, and a loan you just paid off still will.
- **Name mismatches** between passport, residence card and AT record (accents, order, missing surnames) get documents rejected. Fix the AT record first. *(unverified — practice)*
