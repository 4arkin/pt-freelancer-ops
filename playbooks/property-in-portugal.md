# Property in Portugal: renting out, alojamento local, IMI, selling

> **Applies to:** resident trabalhador independente who owns Portuguese property, lets it, runs it as alojamento local, or sells their home
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-02

Owning property adds three tax layers to a freelancer's year. **Rent** is Categoria F (Anexo F), taxed at a flat autonomous rate unless you opt into the brackets. **Alojamento local** (short-term letting) is Categoria B (Anexo B) under the simplified-regime coefficients. **Owning** costs IMI every year, and AIMI above €600,000 of value. Selling your home triggers a capital gain that is excluded if you reinvest. Buying, IMT and the mortgage are in [`mortgage-self-employed.md`](mortgage-self-employed.md). Property abroad: [`foreign-income-and-accounts.md`](foreign-income-and-accounts.md#foreign-rental-income-cat-f-quadro-7a).

## Rules

### Long-term rental — Categoria F, Anexo F

| Item | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Residential lease | **25%** autonomous rate | art. 72.º n.º 2 CIRS (Lei 56/2023) *(official source)* |
| Non-residential lease (shop, office) | **28%** | art. 72.º n.º 1 e) *(official source)* |
| Residential, contract **5–10 years** | 25% − 10 pp = **15%** (each renewal of equal length: −2 pp more, max −10 pp) | art. 72.º n.º 3 *(official source)* |
| Residential, **10–20 years** | **10%** | art. 72.º n.º 4 *(official source)* |
| Residential, **≥ 20 years** | **5%** | art. 72.º n.º 5 *(official source)* |
| **Moderate rent** — residential lease with monthly rent ≤ **€2,300 (2026)**, rents received 2026–2029 | **10%**, unless a lower rate above applies | art. 45.º-C EBF (DL 97/2026) *(official source: EBF text via OCC summary; €2,300 = 2.5 × minimum wage 2026 — secondary: OCC)* |
| Englobamento | optional — then **all** Cat. F income goes to the brackets | art. 72.º n.º 13, art. 22.º n.º 5 *(official source)* |

- **Duration reductions are off** for contracts signed from 1 Jan 2024 whose rent exceeds the municipal price caps by more than 50% (art. 72.º n.º 23). There's an extra **−5 pp** for a new contract whose rent is at least 5% below the previous one on the same property (n.º 24). Ending a reduced-rate contract early, for a reason attributable to you, claws the reduction back with interest (n.º 20). *(official source: art. 72.º CIRS)*
- **Deductible expenses** (per property, paid in the year): maintenance and repairs, condominium charges, **IMI** and **Imposto do Selo** for the let property, rent insurance, and conservation works paid in the **24 months before** the first letting. **Not deductible:** mortgage interest, depreciation, furniture, appliances, décor, **AIMI**. *(official source: art. 41.º n.º 1, 5, 7 CIRS)*
- **Moved more than 100 km away and renting where you live?** The rent you pay is deductible from the rent you receive, if the let property was your fiscal domicile for ≥ 12 months and both leases are registered at AT. *(official source: art. 41.º n.º 8 CIRS)*
- **Withholding:** a tenant with organised accounts (a company) withholds **25%** — **10%** on moderate rents. It's credited against your final tax. *(official source: art. 101.º n.º 1 e), f) CIRS)*
- **Cat. B option:** you can elect to tax the letting as Cat. B instead (art. 4.º n.º 1 n), opted in the início or alterações declaration). On the simplified regime the net rent then counts at 0.95 and goes to the brackets. *(official source: art. 4.º, 31.º n.º 1 d), n.º 4 CIRS)* Worth it only if your marginal rate is under the Cat. F rate. *(unverified)*

### Landlord paperwork

- **Register the lease:** Portal → search "Contratos" → *Comunicar início de contrato* (declaração **Modelo 2**) by the **end of the month after** the lease starts. Changes and termination use the same form and deadline. *(unverified — Montepio, CGD, Santander; AT FAQ confirms the Modelo 2 is mandatory)*
- **Imposto do Selo:** **10% of one month's rent**, billed after the Modelo 2 (on increases, 10% of the increase). *(unverified — CGD, Santander)*
- **Electronic rent receipts:** Portal → Cidadãos → Serviços → Arrendamento → Recibos de renda → *Emitir*. Exempt: landlords aged **65+** on 31 Dec of the prior year, and a few others. If exempt and you don't opt in, file the **Modelo 44** (annual rent statement) by **end of February**. *(official source: AT FAQ faqs-00358)*
- The tenant's IRS rent deduction (Anexo H) depends on your registration and receipts. → [`irs-modelo3.md`](irs-modelo3.md#deductions-à-coleta-anexo-h--e-fatura)

### Alojamento local — Categoria B

- **AL in a moradia or apartamento** is a Cat. B service: coefficient **0.35**, or **0.50 inside a containment area** (*área de contenção*). Hostels and other *estabelecimentos de hospedagem* fall under hotel activity: **0.15**. *(official source: art. 31.º n.º 1 a), c), h) CIRS)*
- **Anexo B** campo **417** (moradia/apartamento) or **416** (hospedagem/hostel). *(official source: Anexo B instructions via OCC)* AL income at 0.35 is also subject to the **15% expense-justification test** (art. 31.º n.º 13) — see [`regime-simplificado.md`](regime-simplificado.md).
- **Per-year option to tax it as Cat. F instead:** Anexo B **quadro 15** — real expenses instead of the coefficient, at Cat. F rates. Campo 01/02 of Q15 is mandatory whenever campo 417 is filled. *(official source: art. 28.º n.º 14 CIRS; Anexo B instructions via OCC)*
- **You need:** the AL registration with the Câmara (RNAL), an added activity code on your Finanças activity, and invoices for every stay, including bookings via platforms (the host invoices the guest, not the platform). *(unverified — AL licensing and IVA treatment not researched for this file; see [`start-activity.md`](start-activity.md) and [`iva-regimes.md`](iva-regimes.md))*
- **Selling an AL property within 3 years** of taking it back out of the activity: the gain is taxed under Cat. B rules. *(official source: art. 10.º n.º 19 CIRS)*

### IMI and AIMI

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| IMI rate — urban property | **0.3% – 0.45%** of the VPT, set yearly by each Câmara | CIMI art. 112.º *(unverified — AT Guia fiscal Comunidades Portuguesas, PwC 2026)* |
| Who pays | owner on **31 December** of the year taxed; paid the following year | *(official source: gov.pt "Pagar o IMI", Sep 2026)* |
| Payment | ≤ €100: **31 May** · €100–€500: **31 May + 30 Nov** · > €500: **31 May + 31 Aug + 30 Nov** (or all at once) | *(official source: gov.pt; oe.gov.pt)* |
| Temporary exemption — own home | **3 years** if VPT ≤ **€125,000** and household income ≤ **€153,300** | *(VPT and 3 years: official source — oe.gov.pt, gov.pt; income limit: unverified — both official pages say €153,000, the EBF limit as introduced by OE 2012 is €153,300 of rendimento coletável; see Gotchas)* |
| IMI familiar | some Câmaras cut €30 / €70 / €140 for 1 / 2 / 3+ dependants, own home only | *(official source: oe.gov.pt)* |
| AIMI | on the **sum** of residential VPTs over **€600,000** per person (€1.2M jointly by option): **0.7%**; 1% above €1M; 1.5% above €2M. Liquidated June, paid in **September** | *(unverified — PwC Guia Fiscal 2026, AICEP)* |

### Selling your home — mais-valias (Anexo G)

- **Taxable gain** = sale price − acquisition value (indexed by the monetary-correction coefficients) − improvement costs from the last 12 years − buying and selling costs. Only **50%** of the balance is taxed, englobado at your marginal rate. *(official source: art. 43.º n.º 2 b) CIRS; Anexo J/G instructions)*
- **Reinvestment exclusion (own and permanent home, art. 10.º n.º 5):** the gain is excluded if the sale price, **minus the mortgage you pay off**, is reinvested in another own-and-permanent home (buying, building, enlarging), in **Portugal or the EU/EEA**, between **24 months before and 36 months after** the sale. Also: the sold home was your **fiscal domicile for the 12 months before**, you declare the intention and the amount in the Anexo G of the sale year, and you move your domicile into the new home **within 12 months** of buying it. Partial reinvestment → partial exclusion. *(official source: art. 10.º n.º 5, 6, 12 CIRS)*
- **New, 2026–2029:** a gain on selling any residential property is also excluded if reinvested in Portuguese housing let at a moderate rent (≤ €2,300/month in 2026). The lease must start within 6 months and run ≥ 36 of the next 60 months. *(official source: art. 10.º n.º 7–9 CIRS, DL 97/2026)*
- **Court delay:** if the purchase falls through and you sue, the reinvestment deadline is suspended while the case runs. *(official source: art. 10.º n.º 27–30 CIRS)*

## How to do it

1. **New lease:** Modelo 2 by the end of the following month → pay the Imposto do Selo → issue an electronic receipt for every rent payment.
2. **AL:** register with the Câmara, add the activity code at Finanças, then decide each year between the coefficient (campo 417) and the Cat. F option (Q15).
3. **Modelo 3:** Anexo F per let property, one row per matrix article/fraction, expenses in the gastos quadro. Simulate with and without englobamento.
4. **IMI:** check *Consultar Património Predial* and the cobrança notes in April–May. Confirm the exemption shows if you claimed one.
5. **Selling the home:** keep the purchase deed, IMT/IS receipts, works invoices from the last 12 years and the mortgage payoff statement. In the sale year's Anexo G, declare the reinvestment intention even if you haven't bought yet.

## Gotchas

- **The 25% is the starting rate, not the only one.** Long leases and moderate rents can bring it down to 5–10% — but only if the lease is registered and its duration is stated.
- **AIMI is not a deductible Cat. F expense,** even though IMI is.
- **AT's IMI FAQ (faqs-00474) still shows the pre-2017 payment months** (April/July/November). Use gov.pt or the cobrança note.
- **Household-income limit for the temporary IMI exemption:** gov.pt and oe.gov.pt say €153,000; the limit enacted in OE 2012 is €153,300 of taxable income. Near the line, read EBF art. 46.º itself. Check the caderneta or the decision to see whether it was granted.
- **Declaring the reinvestment intention is a condition.** Leave it out of the sale year's Anexo G and the exclusion fails even if you buy in time.
- **Furniture for a furnished let isn't deductible in Cat. F.** If it's AL, the coefficient already assumes costs.
- **Non-residents buying housing in Portugal pay IMT at 7.5%** from DL 97/2026, refundable on request if you become resident within 2 years. Buying before your residency starts matters. *(secondary: OCC summary of DL 97/2026; CIMT art. 17.º n.º 10–12 not read)* → [`mortgage-self-employed.md`](mortgage-self-employed.md)
