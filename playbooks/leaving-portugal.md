# Leaving Portugal: close the activity, change tax residence, file the last year

> **Applies to:** a trabalhador independente (regime simplificado) who stops working from Portugal and moves tax residence abroad, EU or non-EU
> **Last verified:** 2026-10-02

Leaving takes three separate acts: **closing the activity** (AT cessação, which also closes SS), **changing tax residence** (address and status at AT, plus a fiscal representative or e-notifications for non-EU destinations), and **filing the last resident year** in the following April–June. Each has its own deadline and none triggers the others, except that SS follows the AT cessação. After that, PPCs and the SS annual review can still produce bills, and AT can reassess for four years (longer in some cases). Keep Portal access and the records.

Opening and changing activity: [`start-activity.md`](start-activity.md). Notifications and debts: [`at-communication.md`](at-communication.md). Certidões: [`certidoes.md`](certidoes.md). IVA refunds: [`iva-regimes.md`](iva-regimes.md).

## Rules

### Order of operations

```
last service ─→ last recibos/faturas ─→ Cessação at AT (≤ 30 days) ─→ SS closes by itself
      │                                        │
      │                                        └→ last IVA DP + recapitulativa · last SS declaração trimestral
      ▼
leave PT ─→ address + status change at AT (≤ 60 days) ─→ representative or e-notifications (non-EU, ≤ 15 days)
      ▼
next Apr–Jun: Modelo 3 for the resident part (Q8C dates) ─→ pay by 31 Aug ─→ keep records 10 years
```

### Cessação de atividade (AT)

- **Deadline:** within **30 days** of the cessation date. *(official source: art. 33.º CIVA; art. 112.º n.º 3 CIRS)* Late or missing: coima **€300–€7,500**. *(official source: art. 117.º n.º 2 RGIT)*
- **The date is a fact,** the day you stop habitually practising the activity, not the day you file. *(official source: art. 114.º n.º 1 a) CIRS; art. 34.º CIVA)*
- **IVA, regime normal:** the cessação doesn't file the last periodic return for you. Deliver the **declaração periódica** for the period containing the cessation date, plus the **recapitulativa** if you had EU B2B services. Sources conflict on the DP deadline: the normal art. 41.º date, or 30 days from cessation. File within 30 days and both readings are met. *(unverified — Dicas Fiscais and its commenters disagree)*
- **IVA credit:** a credit balance can be refunded on cessation (minimum €25). *(official source: art. 22.º CIVA — see [`iva-regimes.md`](iva-regimes.md))*
- **Equipment with IVA deducted** (a laptop bought with IVA reclaimed, say) and kept for personal use counts as a supply: liquidate IVA on it in the last return. *(official source: art. 3.º n.º 3 f) and art. 34.º n.º 1 b) CIVA; practical valuation unverified)*
- **IVA art. 53.º exempt:** no DP to close. Just the cessação. *(official source: art. 53.º CIVA — no periodic return)*
- **Crypto held in the activity:** losing residence counts as a sale for Cat. B crypto income. *(official source: art. 31.º n.º 18 CIRS)*

### Segurança Social

- **Automatic.** SS closes your TI enquadramento from AT's cessação data. There's nothing to file at SS to close. *(official source: ISS FAQ v09, Q50)*
- **The last declaração trimestral** is still due at the next declarative moment (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct). The obligation to contribute ends on the **1st of the month after** cessation. *(official source: ISS FAQ v09, Q14, Q51–52)*
- **The annual review can still bill** differences the following year. *(official source: ISS FAQ v09, Q51)* Debts prescribe after 5 years. *(unverified — art. 187.º CRC, text via a legal publisher)*
- **Moving within the EU and working there:** the new state's SS applies from when you work there (Reg. (EC) 883/2004 art. 11). An A1 is only for temporary postings. See [`social-security.md`](social-security.md#eu-cross-border-the-a1-certificate).

### Tax residence

- **Residence ends on your last day of presence in Portugal.** *(official source: art. 16.º n.º 4 CIRS)* Residence is assessed per person, so a spouse who stays remains resident. *(official source: art. 16.º n.º 5)*
- **Anti-fragmentation rules** — you count as resident for the **whole** departure year if:
  - you spent more than 183 days in PT that year **and** earn income after leaving that isn't taxed in an EU/EEA state, or is taxed elsewhere at less than 60% of the PT rate; *(official source: art. 16.º n.º 14–15 CIRS)*
  - or you become resident again the following year. *(official source: art. 16.º n.º 16)*
- **Tell AT within 60 days** of the change in residence status. *(official source: art. 19.º n.º 5 LGT)* An address change counts at AT only once communicated. *(official source: art. 19.º n.º 4 LGT)* Late update of NIF data: coima €75–€375. *(official source: art. 117.º n.º 4 RGIT)*
- **How, without a Cartão de Cidadão:** since 1 July 2025, address changes go through **e-balcão** or in person by appointment (Portal → Contactos, or +351 217 206 707). Foreign → foreign changes can be submitted on the Portal. *(official source: gov.pt news, 2 Jul 2025)* CC holders change the CC address (ePortugal, Espaço Cidadão or a consulate), which feeds AT. *(unverified — secondary guides)*
- **Leaving for a non-EU/EEA country with any tax relationship left in PT** (open activity, property, PT income, unpaid IRS): name a **fiscal representative** within **15 days** of the address change, **or** join Portal/ViaCTT e-notifications. Joining e-notifications removes the duty. *(official source: art. 19.º n.º 6, 15 LGT; gov.pt "Nomear representante fiscal")* EU/EEA → optional. *(official source: art. 19.º n.º 8 LGT; art. 130.º n.º 2 CIRS)* Cancelling e-notifications while resident outside the EU only works after naming a representative. *(official source: art. 19.º n.º 16 LGT)* Exception: keeping self-employed activity open in PT from a third country needs an **IVA representative**. See [`start-activity.md`](start-activity.md#nif-and-fiscal-representative).

### The last Modelo 3

- **Two statuses in one year → one return per status.** Rosto **Q8A** (resident) with **Q8C** giving the period's start and end dates. A second return for the non-resident period is needed only if that period had PT-source income **not** subject to final withholding (rent, capital gains). *(official source: art. 57.º n.º 6 CIRS; Modelo 3 instructions Q8C; example — APECA guide)*
- **The resident-period return** covers worldwide income of that period: Anexo B (recibos up to cessation), Anexo SS, Anexo J (foreign accounts and foreign income of the period), Anexo A if relevant. Filed **1 Apr – 30 Jun** of the next year, paid by **31 Aug**. See [`irs-modelo3.md`](irs-modelo3.md).
- **PT clients after you leave:** a PT entity paying a non-resident for services withholds **25% final** (art. 71.º n.º 4 a) CIRS). To apply a treaty, give the client **Mod. 21-RFI** plus a residence certificate from your new country *before* payment. Excess already withheld is reclaimed with the RFI refund forms. *(official source: art. 71.º; Mod. 21-RFI form text; refund window "2 years" — unverified, Facilite)*

### Pagamentos por conta

- PPCs for year N are set by the nota for N−2, so they keep arriving after you leave. They stop being due once you **stop earning Cat. B income**. *(official source: art. 102.º n.º 4 b) CIRS)* If stopping or reducing them leaves the departure year more than **20%** short, juros compensatórios apply (n.º 6). AT still sends the payment documents, so keep the cessação comprovativo as your reason for not paying. *(official source for the rule; AT's behaviour after cessação unverified)*

### Records and proof

- **AT can liquidate for 4 years**, counted from the end of the year the income was earned (IVA: from the start of the year after it became due). It's **12 years** for undeclared accounts outside the EU, or undeclared items in tax-haven jurisdictions. *(official source: art. 45.º n.º 1, 4, 7 LGT)*
- **Keep IVA/Cat. B registos and supporting documents for 10 years.** *(official source: art. 52.º CIVA; applied to Cat. B by art. 118.º CIRS)* Keep `records/` whole for 10 years. Keep the IRS comprovativos and notas de liquidação too, since the Portal only shows the last 5 years of comprovativos.
- **Certidão de residência fiscal** for each resident year, in English: request them before you leave. The new country's tax office may need them for split-year treatment, and you need them for treaty relief on income from the PT period. *(official source for the certidão: [`certidoes.md`](certidoes.md); "before you leave" is advice)*

## How to do it

1. **Before the last month:** issue every remaining recibo/fatura dated on or before the cessation date. Collect the e-fatura expense classification for the year (deadline end of February).
2. **Cessação:** Portal → Atividade → Submeter declarações → **Cessação de atividade** → cessation date → IVA reason (usually art. 34.º n.º 1 a/b) → Submeter. Save the comprovativo. *(official source: gov.pt "Entregar declaração de cessação"; path verified in practice — see [`../portals/portal-financas.md`](../portals/portal-financas.md))* Confirm in Situação Fiscal Integrada → Atividade Exercida.
3. **Same month:** last IVA DP (with art. 3.º n.º 3 f) liquidation if needed) and recapitulativa. Request the IVA credit refund if any.
4. **Next Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct:** last SS declaração trimestral. Pay whatever SSD → Posição Atual shows. Save a **declaração de situação contributiva** and a **certidão de não dívida** (AT).
5. **Within 60 days of leaving:** address and status change via e-balcão (Registo Contribuinte → Morada) or in person. Attach proof of the foreign address. *(e-balcão routing unverified)*
6. **Non-EU:** within 15 days, Portal → A Minha Área → Notificações e Citações → Gerir canais → Portal das Finanças → **Ativar** (or name a representative: Serviços → Dados Cadastrais → Representante). See [`start-activity.md`](start-activity.md#how-to-do-it).
7. **Certidões:** Pedir Certidão → Residência Fiscal, one per resident year, in English.
8. **Next April–June:** Modelo 3 with Q8A + Q8C (dates), Anexos B, SS, J. Second return only if needed. Pay by the date on the nota.
9. **Calendar the tail:** PPC months (Jul/Sep/Dec) for two years, the SS annual review, and the 4-year caducidade horizon.

## Gotchas

- **Leaving without the cessação** keeps every obligation running from abroad: quarterly IVA, Anexo B and SS. The cessação coima starts at €300.
- **Filing the cessação with today's date** instead of the real last day of activity puts recibos and IVA in the wrong period.
- **Expecting AT's cessação to file your last IVA return.** It doesn't. The final DP and recapitulativa are separate.
- **Changing the address and deleting the Portal app.** Notifications keep counting as received on day 5 in the Portal box, whether you look or not (see [`at-communication.md`](at-communication.md)).
- **Paying PPCs you no longer owe, or ignoring ones you do.** No Cat. B income → not due. A Cat. B tail in the departure year → check the 20% rule.
- **Coming back next year** makes you resident for the whole departure year (art. 16.º n.º 16). The split-year return becomes wrong.
- **A non-EU move with a property or unpaid IRS left behind** and no representative or e-notifications: coima €75–€7,500 (art. 124.º RGIT), and you can't file a reclamação until it's fixed.
